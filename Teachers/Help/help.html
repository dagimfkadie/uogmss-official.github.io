Help

1. Getting Started

  • To log in, use your assigned credentials (email and temporary password).
  • On first login, you'll be prompted to change your password.
  • Navigate through the homepage depending on your role (Student or Teacher).

2. Changing Your Password

  • Go to the Change Password page.
  • Enter a new password between 6 and 12 characters.
  • Check the "Show Password" box to reveal the input text.
  • Click submit to save your new password.

3. Common Issues & Fixes

  • "Password must be at least 6 characters": Enter a password between 6 and 12 characters.
  • "Failed to change password": Make sure you are logged in properly.
  • "Page not loading": Check your internet and refresh.

4. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  • Can I reuse my old password? No, please choose a new one.
  • What happens after I change my password? You will be redirected to your homepage.
  • What if I close the page? Just log in again using your new password.

5. User Roles

  • Students: Can access their academic data, tasks, and schedules.
  • Teachers: Have tools to manage classes and track student progress.
